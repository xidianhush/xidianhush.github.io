# Dimension

To measure the weight of an object, we need a unit of weight, 
for example, one unit of 1KG. 
To measure the length of an object, we need a unit of length, 
for example, one meter. 
To measure the amount of information of a certain collapsed‑outcome event (sample), 
we also need a unit: an event with a probability of 1/2. 

Measuring an object's property means 
finding a way to express that property using this unit.
